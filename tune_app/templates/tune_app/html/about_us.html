MelodyMatch AI

AI-Powered Music Recommendation Portal is an intelligent web-based application that simplifies music discovery through Machine Learning-based recommendations. By analyzing song attributes and applying similarity-based algorithms such as K-Nearest Neighbours (KNN), the system provides relevant and personalized song suggestions. The application reduces manual searching, improves user engagement, and demonstrates the practical implementation of Machine Learning in recommendation systems. TuneIQ can serve as a useful platform for music streaming applications, entertainment platforms, and digital music libraries seeking intelligent recommendation solutions.

Three steps. No manual searching.

Under the hood, it's a K-Nearest Neighbours model — but here's what it feels like to use.

◌ Play any song

Pick anything from the library — genre, mood, or language, it doesn't matter where you start.

◌ We compare traits

Genre, mood, language, and artist are matched against every other song to find your closest neighbours.

◌ Get matched, transparently

Recommendations show exactly what they matched on — genre, mood, or language — no black box.
